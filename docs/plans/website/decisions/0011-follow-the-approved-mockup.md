# 0011. Follow the approved mockup

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-03
- Deciders: Marco

## Context and Problem

Marco signs off designs before they are built, as with the glyph set.

## Considered Options

- **Build to the approved mockup**
- Design while building

## Decision Outcome

Chosen: **the approved mockup** ([assets/mockup.html](../assets/mockup.html)). Its design comes from the icon: the hero is the icon's sunset scene without its tile (the 12-sided sun sinking behind the horizon line, the bird in front) on jungle green; the beak's bands (cream, amber, orange, black) run along the bottom of the sticky nav and as a short marker above each section heading; cream pages with near-black text; a dark glyph section; status bar rows whose repo names take their glyph's color; the README screenshots in feature cards, with thin strips zoomed onto their Command Center. Copy, the extension ID and store links in the mockup are placeholders.

## Consequences

- Good: no design surprises at review.
- Bad: deviations need Marco's OK again.
