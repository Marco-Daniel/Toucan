Toucan preset palette

The 16 presets from decision 0014, each shown as a Command Center background (text black or white, whichever has more WCAG contrast) and as the status bar glyph and repo name on VS Code's Dark Modern and Light Modern status bars. A ⚠ marks a glyph with less than 3:1 contrast against the status bar.
