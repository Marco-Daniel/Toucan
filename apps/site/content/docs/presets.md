![Pick Preset Color: the toucan-themed palette, each with a swatch in the repository's glyph](preset-color.png)

**Toucan: Pick Preset Color** offers 16 toucan-themed colors, taken from the bird's beak, plumage and jungle. Each comes with a swatch in the repository's [glyph](/docs/glyphs), and the status bar previews the one you're on. The current color is marked, and so is any preset that may be hard to see on the status bar. A picked preset is stored as its hex color, so you can also type it into [Set Color](/docs/colors#set-color) or [`toucan.repos`](/docs/settings#toucanrepos).
